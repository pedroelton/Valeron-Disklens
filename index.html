See what is eating your disk

DiskLens maps your drive, finds the biggest folders and files, detects real duplicates by content, and measures the caches your system can safely rebuild.

  1. Pick a driveChoose the drive that is full and press Scan. A full drive takes 3 to 15 minutes.
  2. Read the diagnosisDiskLens shows where the space went and how much you can get back.
  3. Select and deleteNothing is deleted unless you tick it and confirm. Deleted items go to the Recycle Bin.

Tip: run as administrator to see system folders that are otherwise hidden from the scan.
